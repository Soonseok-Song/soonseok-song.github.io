Prospective students

Graduate students (MS / PhD)

We are a ship hydrodynamics group and computational fluid dynamics is our primary tool. If you are interested in CFD applied to ships — resistance and hull roughness, seakeeping and manoeuvring, ice-going vessels — or in marine renewable energy, we would like to hear from you.

Useful background: fluid mechanics, numerical methods, and some programming. Prior experience with CFD software (STAR-CCM+, OpenFOAM), Python or MATLAB, or towing-tank testing is a plus but not a requirement. Curiosity and persistence matter more than a specific background.

Students are supported to present at domestic and international conferences, and most of our members publish during their degree.

To apply, email s.song@inha.ac.kr with (1) your CV, (2) academic transcripts, and (3) a short paragraph on what you would like to work on and why. Please write Graduate application in the subject line.

Undergraduate researchers

Undergraduate students at Inha University interested in a research experience are welcome to get in touch at any time. No prior experience is required.

Applicants should also complete the formal admission procedure of the Inha University Graduate School; contacting us does not replace it.
